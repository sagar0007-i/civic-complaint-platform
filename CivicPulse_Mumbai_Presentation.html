CivicPulse Mumbai — Smart Civic Grievance Platform

AI-Driven Municipal Complaint Triage & Field Governance for BMC / MCGM
🏛️ BMC Civic Platform

🚨 The Problem in Municipal Governance

  • Citizen Disconnect: Citizens struggle with fragmented helplines and physical ward queues to report urgent civic problems.
  • Manual Routing Bottlenecks: 40%+ of civic complaints in Mumbai are delayed due to manual routing between 24 wards and 7+ specialized departments.
  • Zero Transparency: Citizens have no live tracking, and field staff lack mobile inspection workbenches with proof verification.

🎯 The CivicPulse Solution

  • 100% Digital Redressal: End-to-end platform connecting Citizens, Department Field Officers, and the Municipal Commissioner.
  • Automated AI Triage: Instant category extraction, urgency classification, and direct routing to ward officers.
  • SLA Time Limits: Enforced turnaround times with color-coded risk alarms and auto-escalation.

SLIDE 02 / 06 • SOLUTION ARCHITECTURE

The 4 Core Pillars of CivicPulse

A Unified Multi-Persona Ecosystem for Municipal Operations
Platform Pillars

🏘️ 1. Frictionless Citizen Portal

  • 5-Step Report Wizard: Category picker, AI auto-priority, photo/video uploads, and Leaflet GPS location picker.
  • Live Stepper Tracking: Real-time progress bar from Submitted ➔ In Progress ➔ Resolved with SLA countdown.
  • Duplicate Guard: Prevents spam by detecting nearby identical issues within 100 meters.

👷 2. Department Officer Workbench

  • Department Isolation: Officers only see grievances matching their specific department access code.
  • AI Field Briefing: Summary of civic issue, GPS map location, and citizen evidence photos.
  • Proof-Backed Resolution: Requires mandatory *After-Fix* resolution photo upload before closing.

👑 3. Executive Command Center

  • Citywide KPI Monitoring: Ward-level heatmaps, SLA risk trackers, and officer workload distribution.
  • Automated Escalations: Tickets at risk of SLA breach escalate automatically to Executive Engineers.

📊 4. Public Transparency & Community

  • Open Public Analytics: Live ward leaderboards, average turnaround times, and resolution rates.
  • Community Upvotes: Citizens upvote neighborhood issues to prioritize municipal response.

SLIDE 04 / 06 • ENGINE & ALGORITHMS

Core Services & Intelligent Algorithms

How Business Logic is Executed with Precision and Speed
Core Engine

🤖 1. AI Triage & Entity Extraction

Service: AIService

  • Parses grievance text using NLP heuristics to suggest the exact municipal category and urgency level.
  • Extracts key landmarks and compiles field briefing cards for repair crews.

📍 2. Geo-Spatial Duplicate Detection

Service: DuplicateService

  • Computes Haversine spatial distance (< 100m) and Levenshtein text similarity.
  • Flags redundant reports to merge community upvotes on a single municipal ticket.

⏱️ 3. Dynamic SLA Engine

Service: SLAService

  • Calculates strict completion targets: Critical (12h), High (24h), Medium (48h), Low (72h).
  • Triggers automated alerts when tickets enter the 20% remaining threshold (*At Risk*).

🔀 4. Automated Ward & Dept Routing

Service: RoutingService

  • Evaluates category rules to route tickets to the appropriate department: Solid Waste (SWM), Water Works, Roads, Drains, Lighting, Health, or PWD.

🔄 End-to-End Grievance Lifecycle

  1. Reported: Citizen submits grievance with photo & GPS location.
  2. Triaged: AI classifies severity and routes to the correct ward officer.
  3. Actioned: Officer views ticket in workbench, updates status to In Progress.
  4. Proof Upload: Officer uploads After-Fix photo and logs resolution remarks.
  5. Closed: Ticket moves to Resolved, and citizen receives status update.
